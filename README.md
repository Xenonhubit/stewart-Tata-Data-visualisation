# TATA Data Visualisation Empowering Business with Effective Insights
# Topic Covered
Here are the main topics and concepts covered in this virtual experience programme:
- 📊 Strategic Business Framing and Stakeholder Analysis
- 📈 Visual Analytics Selection and Design Principles
- 🎨 Data-Driven Dashboard Creation using Tableau/Power BI
- 💼 Executive Communication and Insight Presentation**

# Programme Overview
The main purpose of this virtual experience programme is to demonstrate how multidisciplinary data experts at Tata Insights and Quants leverage visualization technologies like Tableau and Power BI to tackle complex business challenges. Through this programme, I aim to showcase the practical application of data visualization in uncovering actionable insights from large-scale retail datasets, closely mimicking real-world consultancy scenarios

# Case-Based Approach
This programme is designed around a realistic business case, where I work as a consultant for an online retail store seeking strategic expansion guidance. By grounding the analysis in an authentic context—complete with CEO and CMO stakeholder perspectives—the programme makes data visualization methodologies and business intelligence techniques more relatable and applicable to actual industry challenges.

# Detailed Analysis
The programme is structured across four comprehensive modules, each building upon the previous to create a complete analytics consulting experience. Each phase, from business requirement gathering to executive presentation, is methodically documented to help others understand not only the "how" but also the "why" behind each analytical decision

# Purpose and Goal
This programme is aimed at providing a detailed, step-by-step exploration of business analytics consulting, from initial stakeholder engagement through final presentation. The goal is to help others grasp the end-to-end process of translating business questions into data-driven insights, even if they are new to visualization tools or business intelligence concepts. This experience is as much about developing strategic thinking as it is about showcasing the technical applications of data visualization platforms.
